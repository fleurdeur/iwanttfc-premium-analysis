If your stream won’t play


CORS. The server must send Access-Control-Allow-Origin for pages on other domains. This is the most common cause.


Expired or wrong URL. Signed URLs expire, and a 404 shows up in the event log with the failing request.


Codec. H.264 with AAC plays everywhere. VP9, HEVC and AV1 depend on the browser and device.


iPhone. Safari supports DASH through ManagedMediaSource only from iOS 17.1, so older iPhones can’t play it.
